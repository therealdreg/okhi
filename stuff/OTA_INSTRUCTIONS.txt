========================================================================
okhi - updating over the air
========================================================================

    okhi_USB_ota.pkg / okhi_PS2_ota.pkg
        ONE file, both chips. This is what you upload.

    ota_esp.bin, ota_rp.bin
        the same two images loose, for updating one chip on its own.

Only for devices already running an OTA capable firmware. A device from
an older release has to be installed once by cable, with
upload_firmware.bat and the .uf2, because the ESP partition table
changed and that cannot be migrated over the air.

Requirement: do not power the device off during an update.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Join the device WiFi network (USB_xxxxxxxxxxxx or PS2_xxxxxxxxxxxx,
password 1234567890) and open http://192.168.4.1/


------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Upload the package
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the UPLOAD FIRMWARE button and pick the .pkg, or:

    curl -X POST -H "Expect:" --data-binary "@okhi_USB_ota.pkg" \
        http://192.168.4.1/ota

That is the whole update. What happens next is automatic:

  a. The ESP checks the package header, its variant tag and both CRC32s.
  b. The ESP half is written to the spare slot and validated.
  c. The RP half is stored on the ESP filesystem.
  d. The ESP reboots into the new firmware.
  e. YOU RECONNECT AND LOAD THE PAGE. This confirms the ESP update.
  f. Only then does the ESP offer the RP image. The RP fetches it over
     SPI, verifies it with a CRC32, backs up its current firmware to a
     golden slot, applies the new one and reboots.
  g. The RP reports back the CRC of what it is running. When it matches,
     the ESP deletes the staged image and the update is closed.

Step (e) is not optional. If nobody loads the page within 5 minutes the
ESP reboots and rolls back to the previous firmware, and the RP is never
touched. An update nobody could reach is an update that failed.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Updating one chip on its own
------------------------------------------------------------------------
    curl -X POST -H "Expect:" --data-binary "@ota_esp.bin" \
        http://192.168.4.1/ota

    curl -X POST -H "Expect:" --data-binary "@ota_rp.bin" \
        http://192.168.4.1/rpimage
    then open http://192.168.4.1/rpcommit

/rpimage only stages the image; /rpcommit tells the RP to apply it.
Use these for development. For releases use the package, which cannot
leave the two chips on mismatched firmware.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
What you give up during an update
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Keyboard capture is suspended for the couple of seconds the SPI transfer
and the commit take, and one packet is lost at the seam when it resumes.
The keyboard itself keeps working normally throughout: those keystrokes
are simply not recorded.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
Checking the result
------------------------------------------------------------------------
http://192.168.4.1/stats reports, among others:

    partition        ESP slot in use, ota_0 or ota_1
    booted_pending   1 when this session came from an OTA
    rp_image_ready   1 while an RP image is still staged
    ring_dropped     must stay at 0
    spi_records      rises as you type, matches the RP's own counter


------------------------------------------------------------------------
If something goes wrong
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power loss while the ESP half is uploading or being written is harmless:
it is written to the inactive slot and the old firmware keeps running.

A new ESP firmware that boots but cannot be reached rolls back on its
own after 5 minutes.

A new RP firmware that boots but cannot talk to the ESP is replaced by
the golden backup after three attempts.

The one step with no software recovery is the couple of seconds while
the RP is rewriting its own application. If power is lost exactly there,
the RP comes up as a USB mass storage device and has to be recovered by
copying okhi.uf2 onto it.
